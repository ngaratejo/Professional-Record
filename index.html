05Proof of impact

When the numbers tell the story.

Each case opens into the challenge, what I did and what changed for the business.

The challenge

Phone CSAT for a premium line of business had remained around 4.40 for more than two years, consistently below the 4.60 target.

The diagnosis

I used leadership-level outlier management, performance data and coaching paths to identify the drivers behind the gap and determine where intervention would have the greatest impact.

The action

  • Identified performance patterns and outliers
  • Strengthened leadership accountability
  • Created targeted coaching paths
  • Connected operational drivers with customer experience outcomes
  • Established consistent follow-up mechanisms

The result

4.40 → 4.63

Target exceeded by Q4 2025, 1 Q after the assignment of this LOB under my scope. Performance was subsequently sustained at 4.65+ throughout 2026.

Business impact

38% → 50%

Following sustained Green Scorecard performance, market share allocation moved from 38% to 50%. The sustained performance contributed to increased scope allocation and strengthened client confidence.

The challenge

Fleet Partner CSAT had historically remained around 3.5 or below for more than three years. The operation supported high-value Mexican fleet partners, including accounts with 100+ vehicles/drivers, making the customer journey particularly complex.

The diagnosis

The investigation identified a significant issue with survey response and understanding. The challenge was not simply the service interaction itself. There was an opportunity to improve how customer feedback was being captured.

The action

I brought best practices from Delivery, analyzed the customer feedback mechanism and proposed a survey experience improvement.

The result

12% → 23%

Survey response rate. CSAT moved to 4.0+ vs. a 3.78 target.

Business impact

The change created greater visibility into customer sentiment and strengthened the foundation for CX improvement across the broader vertical.

The problem

More than $50K in monthly leakage associated with refund and appeasement decisions.

The solution

I co-developed a calculator with Innovation to standardize decision-making, improve process adherence and reduce unnecessary leakage.

The result

48% → 92%

Process adherence.

The impact

The solution became the first client-approved tool deployed across the network.

The challenge

Multiple Mobility LOBs were operating across different customer journeys, creating opportunities that were difficult to identify from an individual-LOB perspective.

The action

During a high-value visit with the Head of CX for Mobility, I presented the End-to-End Mobility Framework connecting 10+ LOBs and 4 customer journeys.

The result

8

Initial opportunities identified across Customer Experience, Cost, Efficiency and Process improvement.

The impact

The framework created a broader way of looking at Mobility, moving from individual operational performance toward connected customer journeys and cross-functional opportunities.
